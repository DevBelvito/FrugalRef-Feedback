# Privacy Policy for FrugalRef

**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

FrugalRef ("the App") is a desktop reference board application built with a strict commitment to user privacy. This policy explains our "offline-first" approach and how your data is protected.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
**FrugalRef does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, telemetry, or analytics.**

The Application operates entirely offline on your local machine. We do not track how you use the Application, which features you interact with, or any identifying information about your device.

## 2. File and Media Processing
All canvas operations—including importing images and videos, arranging and grouping items, drawing, cropping, and saving scenes—are executed locally on your device's hardware.
* **Zero Uploads:** At no point are your images, videos, or saved `.frugalref` scene files uploaded to external servers, cloud providers, or third-party services.
* **Local Storage:** The Application only reads and writes files to the directories you explicitly choose on your local drives, including the media you drag onto the canvas and the scene files you save or open.
* **No Account Required:** The App does not require you to create a user account to use any feature.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Network Access
FrugalRef does not require an active internet connection to function. However, the Application contains optional links to external platforms for community support and feedback:
* **GitHub:** For submitting bug reports and feature requests.
* **Ko-fi:** For voluntarily supporting the development of the Application.

If you choose to click these links, your web browser will open these respective platforms. FrugalRef does not share any data with these platforms. Please refer to their individual privacy policies for information on how they handle your data when you visit their sites.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If our practices change in the future (for example, if new features require optional network permissions), we will update this Privacy Policy and clearly communicate those changes within the Application's release notes. However, our commitment to never collecting your personal data or uploading your files will remain absolute.
